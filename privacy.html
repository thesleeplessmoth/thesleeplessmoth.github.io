Privacy policy

SleeplessMoth Uploader · last updated 19 November 2026

What this app is

SleeplessMoth Uploader is a private tool used only by SleeplessMoth to upload and schedule its own videos on its own YouTube channels (SleeplessMoth and After Happily Ever After). It is not offered to the public.

What data it uses

When the channel owner signs in, the app receives permission from Google to manage that owner's own YouTube videos, playlists, thumbnails and captions. It uses this permission only to upload, schedule and update SleeplessMoth's own videos.

What it does not do

It does not collect, sell or share any personal data. It does not access other people's accounts or videos. The sign-in token is stored only on SleeplessMoth's own computer and is never sent anywhere except to Google.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account page under Security, Third-party apps.
